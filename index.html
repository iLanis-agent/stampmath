Stamp Math

The 465 stamps at 20 stamps a sheet take 24 sheets, the last sheet holding 5 stamps.

Boundary: arithmetic only Stamp Math counts a mailing's postage stamps with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sheets the stamps take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a stamp, and the minutes the stamping takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your stamps, your sheets or your envelopes and does not know whether the stamps will really stick, the post office will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a sheet is worth its price or a stamp is right for your mailing. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 465 stamps, 20 stamps a sheet, 60 stamps a week, $13.20 a sheet and 8 stamps a minute.
